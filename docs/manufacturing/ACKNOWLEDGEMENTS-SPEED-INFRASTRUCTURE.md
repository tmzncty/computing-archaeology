# Acknowledgements — Speed Infrastructure Field Set

This field set is intentionally about work that tends to disappear behind the word “performance.”

It owes its existence to people who make speed repeatable rather than merely demonstrable:

- EUV resist chemists, pellicle membrane teams, reticle inspectors, e-beam/metrology engineers, scanner field service, stochastic-yield analysts;
- backside-wafer thinning, bonding, nano-TSV, buried-power-rail, power-integrity, PDN extraction and physical-design teams;
- nanosheet/forksheet/CFET integration, selective etch, spacer, contact, stress-engineering and process-control teams;
- HBM DRAM, TSV, base-die, microbump/bond, stack assembly, burn-in, memory-interface and package-yield teams;
- hybrid-bond surface prep, CMP, wafer/die bonding, alignment, particle inspection, die sort, KGD logistics and failure-analysis teams;
- silicon-photonics, optical-engine packaging, laser, fiber attach, optical connector, CMIS/management and field-service teams;
- SerDes, package/PCB channel, connector, cable, equalization, FEC, compliance and high-bandwidth test-instrument teams;
- busbar, high-voltage connector, protection, converter, rack-integration, commissioning and electrical-safety teams;
- coolant-chemistry, corrosion, filtration, CDU controls, pump, QD, leak-detection, water-treatment and maintenance teams;
- collective-algorithm, switch silicon, firmware, MPI/NCCL runtime, network-management and telemetry teams.

The historical lesson is that “faster” almost always means somebody had to build a new maintenance discipline around a bottleneck that previously did not deserve a name.
