## Invisible labor principle

A post-scaling computer works only if thousands of interfaces remain inside their windows at once.

The people maintaining those windows belong in computing history:

```text
mirror clean enough
reticle aligned enough
nanosheet released enough
bond surface flat enough
TSV stack good enough
interposer routed enough
PAM4 eye recoverable enough
retimer trained enough
busbar cool enough
quick disconnect dry enough
```

Their best result is often that nothing dramatic happens.
