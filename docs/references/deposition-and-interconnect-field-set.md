# Deposition and Interconnect Field Set — Source Map

This source map supports the excavation round connecting **thin-film source materials, deposition chemistry, lithographic amplification, copper interconnect, chamber process parts, CMP conditioning, and PCB metallization interfaces**.

It is a research map, not a substitute for claim-level article footnotes.

## Source-handling rules for this field set

1. **Patents are primary technical disclosures, not neutral market histories.** They are useful for mechanism, vocabulary, and dated engineering problems.
2. **Corporate research histories are strong for internal development but not universal priority claims.** IBM copper and chemically amplified resist accounts are explicitly labeled as IBM-centered evidence.
3. **Later industry reviews are used for stable process description, not projected backward as early specifications.**
4. **PCB trade/IPC papers describe mature practice and retrospective process lineage.** First-use claims should be corroborated when contemporary board-house records are available.
5. **Experiments are synthetic.** They expose constraint structure and do not reproduce commercial recipes, material specifications, or safety limits.

## 11. Preservation targets specific to this round

This field set is especially vulnerable because many important objects are consumables or maintenance parts. Preserve evidence for:

```text
spent sputter targets
backing plates / target bonds
precursor bottles / ampoules / bubblers
PAG/resist formulation records
plating bath analysis sheets
barrier / seed deposition recipes
ESC refurbishment records
used ceramic wafer stages
O-rings with known chamber service
CMP pads and diamond conditioners
surface-finish bath control records
microvia cross-sections
```

A used process part can contain historical information that a schematic cannot:

- erosion pattern;
- deposition buildup;
- plasma attack;
- thermal discoloration;
- mechanical wear;
- corrosion;
- residue;
- repair marks.

## Final caution

This field set crosses many decades and industries. Terms such as `ALD`, `ESC`, `ENIG`, `superfill`, and `chemically amplified resist` refer to evolving technology families.

Do not silently treat a modern implementation as if it were present in the earliest version of the idea.

The repository's rule remains:

> **date the evidence, identify the source type, and keep historical record separate from engineering reconstruction.**
